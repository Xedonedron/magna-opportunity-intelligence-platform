## 💡 Slide 2: The Idea
**From Fragmented WhatsApp Chats to AI-Powered Sales Intelligence**

* **The Challenge:** Sales engineers waste 2-3 hours manually researching prospective clients before meetings, struggling with scattered WhatsApp notes and undocumented technical knowledge.
* **The Innovation:** MOIP automates Know-Your-Customer (KYC) research by combining live web intelligence with RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) against Smartnet Magna's internal solution catalog, powered by Google's **Gemma 4** (`gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`).
* **The Impact:** Engineers arrive at client meetings fully prepared with executive summaries, tailored pain points, and pre-matched Smartnet Magna solutions.

## 🔥 Slide 4: Key Features
* 🤖 **Automated AI KYC Research:** Instant company profiling, industry analysis, business model, and news synthesis in < 5 minutes.
* 🎯 **RAG Solution Recommender:** Automatically matches customer pain points with Smartnet Magna's internal PDF solution datasheets and Google Cloud products.
* 📝 **KYC Versioning & Editor:** Tracks immutable report revisions (`v1` Auto, `v2` Regenerated, `v3+` Engineer-edited) with full diff support.
* 📊 **Executive Pipeline Analytics:** Real-time metrics, 30-day conversion trends, engineer workload, and role-based views.
* 📅 **Meeting Manager & Google Integration:** Direct Google Calendar event creation, participant invites, and automated email reminders.
* 🔒 **Role-Based Access Control & Audit Logs:** Secure RBAC (Admin, Manager, Presales Engineer, LGO) with complete audit trail logging.
