## Halaman 23: Use Case: Security Management Solutions

### 1. Use Case: EPM for Corporate Environment
- **Skenario**: In a corporate environment, employees need to stay productive, IT teams must keep systems secure, and third-party vendors may require limited, temporary access.

#### How EPM Works:
- **Remove Local Admin Rights**: Standard users no longer have full admin privileges, reducing malware and unauthorized system changes.
- **Just-In-Time Privilege Access**: Temporary elevated access is granted only for specific tasks when needed.
- **Smart Application Control**: Trusted apps run automatically, while unknown or risky apps are blocked, sandboxed, or approved once.

#### Outcome:
Stronger endpoint security with minimal impact on daily operations.

---

### 2. Use Case: PAM for Banking Assets
- **Skenario**: Banks manage numerous privileged accounts with access to critical financial systems.

#### How PAM Secures Privileged Access:
- **Automated Password Management**: Privileged passwords are securely stored and automatically rotated.
- **Real-Time Session Monitoring**: All sessions are monitored and recorded, with suspicious activity stopped instantly.
- **Complete Audit Trail**: Every action is logged in a tamper-proof record for compliance and investigations.

#### Outcome:
Controlled privileged access, reduced insider risk, and stronger regulatory compliance.
